## Bunny Beat Down v0.8.0: the rogues have voices

**Every rogue talks.** All 49 rogues now say their taunts out loud as well as in speech bubbles, each in a voice of its own that fits its animal and personality: hamsters squeak, squirrels chatter, frogs croak, beavers rumble, the nobles (Sir Reynard, Lady Garbage, Sir Toadsworth...) are frightfully British, hotheads gabble, sneaks whisper and the calm ones take their time. They call you by name, read out the score when it matters, shout at their rivals and the director, and have something to say about your outfit.

Voices come from where the rogue stands, so you can hear one coming. There's a new **Voice volume** slider in Settings.

The voices were synthesized with Kokoro-82M (Apache 2.0), credited in the game.
