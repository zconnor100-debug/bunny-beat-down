## Bunny Beat Down v0.18.0: Old Hoot, the Night Watch

**A fourth boss, and the first one that flies.** Old Hoot is the island's other Boss Battle, by night: pick her in the new **Boss** row when you set up a Boss Battle on Wildwood Island. A snowy owl fifty metres wing tip to wing tip, with a sliver of the Starfall Acorn glowing gold-green in her left eye, she circles the fight up high, silent, and comes down out of the dark for it.

**What she does:**
- **Snatch.** A red ring on whoever she's after, and she drops out of the sky onto it. Anyone still in it is carried off in her talons and dropped from high up. Caught? Jump, and keep jumping, and you'll wriggle free; or everyone else shoots her till she lets go.
- **Talon Rake.** A low pass along a red strip, talons out.
- **Feather Volley.** Hovering, she flings a flurry of big feathers onto red rings.
- **Hoot.** She puffs up and hoots, and everyone in the ring under her is knocked flat.
- **Perch.** Now and then she comes down to rest, low and still. Go for the eye.
- **Grounded.** Shoot her wings while she's diving and she comes down hard, stunned, taking double.

**Her arrival** is a cinematic like the others': she glides in out of the dark, lands, and hoots for the camera. The rogues on your side know her ("It's Old Hoot! And we're still out past the first hoot!"), tell you to shoot her wings, and yell when she's carrying one of them off, in all 49 of their voices.

**Her story**, four chapters on the Trophy Room's Bosses page: the night watch the Warren's kits are warned about at bedtime, the sliver of the Acorn in her eye, how to ground her, and why she flies so high since the Acorn went home.

**Rewards:**
- **Past the First Hoot** (beat Old Hoot): the **Feathers** beat-down burst.
- **The Big Four** (beat all four bosses): 400 acorns.
- The Warren Gazette's special edition: OLD HOOT GROUNDED FOR GOOD.

**Brutus has a face again.** His model lost its sculpted detail when it was cut down for the game, and at ten critters tall it showed: a flat, dark face and pinprick eyes. Now the full sculpt's detail is baked back into him (a normal map, and its creases darkened into his skin), his skin isn't a mirror any more (it was reflecting the sky, which turned him blue-grey), and his own amber eyes glow, the way Nibbles's do.

**Ragdolls keep their shape.** A critter beaten down now crumples as it is. Before, its ragdoll stretched it out, a hamster pulled out long and a frog's legs out straight, its joints dragging it back toward the shape it was modelled in (and a squirrel's legs fell away from it altogether).

**Also:**
- Four new sounds, made in code: her hoot, her screech as she dives, her wingbeats, and the Feather Volley.
- A burrow caved in in Act III no longer leaves its tube's collision squashed out of shape.
- The Warren Gazette prints a photo taken by night as clearly as one taken by day (it was nearly all ink).
- Her model is the Gliding Snowy Owl (Pigcraft, CC BY 4.0), rigged with her wings on its arms' bones and her talons on its feet's.
