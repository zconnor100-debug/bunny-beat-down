## Bunny Beat Down v0.16.0: the Kernel Hunt's Act III, Foam at the Falls

**Act III of the Kernel Hunt is playable, in the hamster cage.** Both Kernels pull at the cage, so the Barn Rangers follow them in, over the glass and down into the bedding. Under it Nibbles has dug his tunnels toward the toy room, toward the hum, and his water bottle has never once run dry: the third Kernel, no bigger than a seed, is in it. It opens on the Kernel Hunt's page once the Clockwork Decree is done.

**Cave in his burrows.** His burrows are dug into the hamster's heaps of bedding, and the Touched keep scrabbling up out of them while you're near. Nothing you can shoot shuts one: a blast in its mouth does, and the heap caves in over it. You start with grenades, a crate brings more (and a rocket launcher), and the rangers' rockets count too.

**Dam Water Bottle Falls.** Carry chew blocks one at a time, over your head and slowly, into the ring under the bottle's spout while the Touched come at you. Get knocked off your feet and you drop it, to be picked up again. With three in, the last drips stop and the puddle drains away.

**Something wants a drink.** The bedding by the dam heaves, and Nibbles bursts up out of it.

**The ending.** When he falls, a gold-green light swells in his cheeks and he shrinks in a froth of foam until he's a little golden hamster with very full cheeks: the children's Nibbles, a good boy. He squeaks and spits out the third Kernel. "He's... tiny." "Nibbles? Good boy. Who's a good boy?" Buck Toothington brings out the other two, the three find each other in the air and turn round each other, brighter and brighter, and then the Briar comes up through the floor all round them. To be continued in the Finale. Twelve new lines, in every rogue's own voice.

**Rewards:**
- **The Third Kernel** (beat Nibbles in Act III): the **Sunflower seeds** beat-down burst.
- **Nobody Foamed** (finish Act III with all seven rangers standing): 300 acorns.
- The Warren Gazette's special edition: NIBBLES IS A GOOD BOY AGAIN.
