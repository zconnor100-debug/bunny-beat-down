## Bunny Beat Down v0.15.0: knocked off their feet, and toys that come apart

**Knockdowns.** A hard hit now throws a critter into a real ragdoll tumble instead of just making it flinch: a rocket or a grenade going off nearby, a sniper's carrot, a shotgun's whole load close up, a club swung hard, a boss's blow, or the toy train. It tumbles, rolls and lands wherever the hit sends it, then about a second later springs back up and fights on. You can shoot critters while they're down, and they can be beaten down for good. Lighter hits shove a critter back, and a hit on a bot's shoulder turns it round, its aim with it. You're only knocked down by blasts, bosses and the train, never by a bot's carrots.

**Ragdolls start from the pose you see.** Every ragdoll, knocked down or beaten down, used to start from the model's rest pose, arms out in a T, for a moment before flopping. Now it starts exactly as the critter stood.

**Block Castle comes apart.** It's built block by block now. A rocket or a grenade against a wall knocks out the blocks it reaches, they tumble and stay where they land, and anything that was standing on them comes down too. Bosses' stomps and blows break it as well: the Tin Tyrant can walk through his own castle.

**Domino Run.** The dominoes stand closer together and topple for real. Shoot one, or blast it, and it goes over into the next, and that one into the next, all the way round the run.

**Look out below.** A toy block or a domino coming down hard on a critter hurts it and knocks it off its feet.
