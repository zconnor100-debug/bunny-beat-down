## Bunny Beat Down v0.7.4: they noticed your outfit

**The rogues notice what you're wearing.** Show up in a wizard's hat or a cheese hat and they'll have something to say about it, when they spot you, when they beat you down and when you beat them down: every wardrobe piece has its own lines, plus a few for anything you put on.

**Four new pieces to earn** (they can't be bought):
- the **snorkel mask**, for winning a match on Wildwood Island
- the **cheese hat**, for winning in the Hamster Cage
- a **wind-up key** on your back, for winning in Toy Box Bash
- the **laurel wreath**, for beating down your Nemesis

**Your outfit in the final shot.** The replay notes what you were wearing ("Dressed to win: your wizard's hat and pixel shades"), and the end screen shows you on a little turntable in your outfit, holding the gun you finished with.
