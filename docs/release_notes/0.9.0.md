## Bunny Beat Down v0.9.0: Epic graphics, made for wide screens

**A new Epic quality tier**, above Ultra: light shafts through the trees and haze (volumetric fog), finer detail on distant models, denser grass and flowers, ultra-soft shadows that reach further, and higher-quality ambient occlusion and light bounce. Ultra and Epic both add **screen-space reflections** on water, glass and shiny toys.

**Your choice of anti-aliasing and resolution**: FXAA, SMAA, TAA, MSAA (2×, 4× or 8×, smoothed by SMAA) or FSR 2 as native anti-aliasing; and **FSR 2 upscaling** (Quality, Balanced, Performance) for big screens and smaller GPUs, or 150% supersampling for the sharpest picture. Plus window mode (maximized, borderless fullscreen, exclusive fullscreen, windowed), V-Sync and a frame-rate cap.

**Wide screens**:
- **Screen shape**: fill the screen, or play in standard 16:9 or ultrawide 21:9 with black bars, whichever you prefer.
- **HUD width**: keep the health, minimap and weapons in a 16:9 or 21:9 column instead of the far corners of a super-wide monitor.
- **Super-ultrawide view**: past 21:9 the view stops getting wider, so the edges don't stretch (or turn it off for the full width).

**If your graphics card draws things wrongly** (blocky or missing textures), Settings → **Graphics API** switches to Direct3D 12 or the Compatibility renderer on the next start, and the settings screen shows which API and graphics card the game is using.

**Drop faster**: while gliding down, press Jump to let go of your glider and fall fast, and Jump again to open it whenever you like. After you've let go, it only opens by itself just above the ground.

Settings are now grouped (Controls, Graphics, Display, Game, Audio) and scroll on small screens.
