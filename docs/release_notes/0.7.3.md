## Bunny Beat Down v0.7.3: tidier squirrels, snug hats, proper capes

**Squirrels take after Kernel**: every squirrel's eyes are now dark and glossy in their sockets (no more pale, sunken look on the lighter furs), and hats sit bigger and lower on a squirrel's big head, the way Kernel's straw hat does.

**Hats fit beavers**: a beaver's tall head is now properly covered by hard hats, beanies, bucket hats, the Mountie's hat and the knight's helmet, instead of poking out of the top.

**Bandanas are tied on**: the knot and tails sit snug at the back of the head and neck instead of hanging in the air behind.

**Capes reach the ground**: capes hang full length from the shoulders, are wide enough to cover the back, and drape over the root of a tail rather than stopping short above it.

**Short arms, sensible carry**: hamsters (and squirrels and raccoons) hold a relaxed gun at their tummy instead of stretching their little arms down to a muzzle by their feet; long-armed critters keep their low carry.
