## Bunny Beat Down v0.13.0: the Kernel Hunt's Act I, The Carrot Patch

**Act I of the Kernel Hunt is playable.** The tally on the Big Red Barn's door says nought, and the Barn Rangers want to know what the Touched came out of the Carrot Patch for. It opens on the Kernel Hunt's page once you've held the Barn in the prologue.

**Track the glowing carrots.** Five of them on a trail from the Barn out into the Carrot Patch, the Acorn's gold-green light shining out of them in crawling veins. One glows bright at a time, its light going up over the grass (it's marked on your screen and the minimap, with how far it is). Hold interact by it to pull it up, and the next one lights. The Touched come up out of the dirt round each one.

**Burn the Patch.** Three rings in the Patch: stand in one, with none of the Touched in it, until it catches (faster with the rangers in it with you; not at all while one of the Touched is in it), while they rush you from the Boulder Bluff side. The fires are new: flames heaped up in the middle, dark smoke climbing, embers, a flickering light and a crackle. With all three alight the whole Patch burns, and the ground starts to drum.

**Wake him at Boulder Bluff.** Get close and Brutus comes down off the Bluff for you, with his arrival cinematic, and the Warren answers: Cottontail Kate, Hops McGee, Acorn Al, Ember, Rocco, Bog Boss, Timber and Cheeks McGee saw the smoke, and come gliding in to fight him with you.

**The ending.** When he falls, a gold-green light swells in his chest and he shrinks, smaller and smaller in his own dust, until what's lying there is Little Bru, the runt of the Patch warren, grey like the giant he was. Over him hangs what was inside him: the first Starfall Kernel, humming. "It's... it's just a bunny." "Little Bru? Is that you?" He looks up at it, the way he used to look at the stars. "It's humming. Like it's calling something. Across the lake." Fifteen new lines, in every rogue's own voice.

**Rewards:**
- **The First Kernel** (beat Brutus in Act I): the **Patch fire** beat-down burst, embers and a puff of smoke.
- **Nobody Singed** (finish Act I with all seven rangers standing): 300 acorns.
- The Warren Gazette's special edition: BRUTUS IS LITTLE BRU AGAIN, with the photo.

**Also:**
- Three new sounds, made in code like the rest: a fire's crackle, the whoomph of a ring catching, and the Kernel's hum.
- Brutus beaten in Act I counts toward his records and opens his story's chapters, as in a Boss Battle.
- Bots plan paths faster still: holding a post or coming for a flare's crate no longer searches the whole map's navmesh for where to stand.

The story so far, and where it goes, is in docs/LORE.md.
