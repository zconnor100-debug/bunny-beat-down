## Bunny Beat Down v0.6.1: critters that move like they mean it

**Watch the new 30-second trailer** (`trailer_phone.mp4` below, made for phones): 30-critter brawls on all three maps, the toy train ploughing through a battle, a rocket into a huddle in slow motion, mutated carrots, a nemesis and its gang, and the rewind.

### Animation
- **Idle life.** Critters standing about breathe (and pant after a sprint), shift their weight, glance around, and do their own species' fidgets: a bunny's warning thumps, a squirrel's nibble, tail swishes, stretches, ear scratches, a look over the gun.
- **Guns carried properly.** Up on the aim when shooting, aiming, reloading or just hit; lowered when things go quiet; swung across the body at a sprint; held clear of big cartoon heads (the beaver's launcher rides on its shoulder). Shots still leave from where the raised gun would point.
- **Paws that really hold the guns.** Fists close round the grips and the other paw takes the fore-end, for every critter with every gun. Mitten paws (the frog, the raccoon, the beaver) are given knuckles so they can close too.
- **Movement and jumps.** A real lean into a run, arms pumping, hoppers pitching on each bound, feet stepping round when you turn on the spot, a kick-off and a tuck in the air, landings that sink and bounce back.

### Updates from now on
New versions are built and published automatically whenever the game changes, and the main menu tells you when one is out, with a button to get it (Settings → "Check for updates" turns it off).
