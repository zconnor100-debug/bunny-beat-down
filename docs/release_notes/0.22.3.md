## Bunny Beat Down v0.22.3: carrot grenades go off in a fireball, and carrots don't pile up in critters

**Carrot grenades hit much harder.** The blast reaches 9 metres instead of 6, and does 130 damage at the middle instead of 80. One of yours that lands beside a critter now beats it down unless it wears an acorn shield; it used to leave it on about 40. A critter 6 metres off still loses about half its health. Bots won't lob one at anyone closer than 10 metres, so they keep out of their own blast.

**Explosions go off in a real fireball.** They used to be a quick beige puff. Now a blast starts with a white-hot flash, then a fireball of cartoon puffs burns orange and dark red, and black smoke climbs out of it as the fire dies down. It also throws a ring of dust out along the ground, along with carrot shrapnel and clods of dirt, and the flash is brighter. Rockets, the Carrot Lobber and the bosses' blasts use the same effect at their own sizes.

**A critter only holds six carrots now.** Every carrot that hit a critter used to stay in it for good. Bots soften their shots at each other, so a kill takes a lot of hits, and in a 49-bot match one critter was carrying 39 carrots five seconds after the landing. Now:

- A critter holds six at a time (a boss 24). Each new one knocks the oldest out: it hops out backwards, drops to the ground, lies there a moment and shrinks away.
- A critter that's still going works its carrots out after about ten seconds. A corpse keeps the ones in it until it's gone.
- In first person, the carrots stuck in your own bunny are hidden along with its body, so they no longer show in your view.
- Stuck carrots more than 40 metres from the camera aren't drawn; at that distance they were a pixel or two.

On speed: stuck carrots never cost much. Measured on Low with every bot in the match, hiding every stuck carrot saved about 0.03 ms a frame (under 1%), both before this change and after it. The pile-up was a problem with how it looked, not with speed.

**v0.22.3.1 fixes:**

- Carrots that stick in the ground, a tree or a wall no longer log an error each time. v0.22.3 logged 100 to 300 of them a match.
- Pack Sense's x-ray glow always wears off on time. If it came on just after a hitch, it could stay on a critter for good.
- Finishing a run's match no longer logs an error for each of your guns.
- Test runs and benchmarks keep their logs in their own folder, so they don't push the game's own logs out.

Measured again with the development PC at full speed, v0.22.3 runs as fast as v0.22.2, about 205 to 220 frames a second in a 49-bot fight at Low. Over its life the new fireball costs the graphics card the same as the old explosion; only its first frames right in front of the camera cost a fraction of a millisecond more.
