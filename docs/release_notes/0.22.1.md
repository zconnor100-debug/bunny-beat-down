## Bunny Beat Down v0.22.1: Old Hoot hits harder and falls flat, and the squirrel's left eye sits right

**Old Hoot goes down flat.** Shot out of the sky, or toppling where she stands, she hits the ground on her front with her wings flung out and splayed flat across it, her head on its side, a last shudder through her wingtips, and lies there. She used to end up slumped half upright with her wings folded.

**Old Hoot is harder.** A whole side could get through her battle without losing anyone. Now:

- **What she carries off doesn't get up again.** She climbs higher before she lets go, and a bot she drops from up there is out whatever the difficulty (your own fall still only hurts).
- **She holds on.** The bots' shots hardly loosen her grip now; it takes yours. Once she's enraged she isn't letting go for them at all, and you have to wriggle harder to get free.
- **Her Feather Volley rains on the crowd**, not just on whoever she's after: more feathers, one for each critter round them, hitting harder.
- She rests less between moves, perches less often, and her Talon Rake hurts more.

Measured over four 90-second battles a setting, she now beats down about five critters on Scrappy and on Sleepy (on Sleepy she used to manage none).

**The squirrel's left eye** sits up in its socket now, its top tucked under the brow, level with the right one. A fix in v0.17.0 had moved the wrong eye; the left one sat low with the top of its socket showing over it.
