## Bunny Beat Down v0.20.0: Noodle, the Burrow Hunter

**The Hamster Cage has a second boss.** Pick her in the main menu's Boss row with the Hamster Cage and a Boss Battle: Noodle, the House's ferret, sable brown with a black bandit's mask and violet eyes. She found the tunnels Nibbles dug under the bedding, ate his whole larder (every seed soaked in water from the bottle that never runs dry), and grew as long as the Big Wheel is wide. Ferrets were bred to go down rabbit holes. Every map now has two bosses.

- **Pop Goes the Weasel.** She digs in, flinging bedding at whoever's behind her, and goes under. A music box plays the old rhyme under the bedding as she comes, and on **POP!** she bursts up out of a red ring under whoever she's after (out of one of the hamster's burrows, if there's one near), three times over (five once she's angry). Get out of the ring before the POP.
- **Bop her on the nose.** Up out of her hole she stops to look round, and her nose counts three times over. Bop it enough and she's seeing stars: flat on the bedding, taking double.
- **Weasel War Dance.** Back arched, mouth open, dooking, she hops sideways at you, each landing on its own red ring. Then she's danced herself out and **sleeps like the dead**, taking double. Hit her, and get clear before she wakes in a **poof of musk** that hangs there and stings.
- **Lunge.** Up close she coils back and strikes along a red strip, jaws first.

She's long and low: ten critters tall only when she rears up on her hind legs. The model is the Ferret by SDPM Esare (CC BY 4.0); her mouth was modelled shut, so the build now splits a mouth along its lips, and her jaw opens on her fangs and tongue. Every rogue has three new lines in their own voice: "It's Noodle! Somebody stop that MUSIC!", "When she pops up, bop her on the nose!" and "She's out cold! Hit her NOW!". She has her own four chapters on the Trophy Room's Bosses page and a Gazette special edition when she falls, and seven sounds made in code: the music box's rhyme (twice round, and once), a ferret's dooking, her hiss, a snore, frantic digging and the poof.

**Beat her for Finders Keepers**, a beat-down burst of her stash (buttons, bottle caps, scraps of odd socks, and a glint of something gold-green), and **The Big Six**: beat all six bosses for 600 acorns (The Big Five is now any five of them).
