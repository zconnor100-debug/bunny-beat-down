## Bunny Beat Down v0.7.0: the rogues get dressed

**Every one of the 49 rogues now has their own look**, the same every match, picked to match who they are: Captain Carrot leads the charge in a captain's cap and medal, Sir Toadsworth wears a top hat and monocle, Dam Dan a hard hat, Sly the tracker a deerstalker, Bunnicula a cape, Timber a lumberjack beanie, Hazel an archer's cap, Croaker a cowboy hat. Each also has its own fur. Over forty hats, glasses, masks, scarves, capes and backpacks are fitted to all eight species and ride on the bones. Land a headshot and the hat goes flying. Your own critter keeps the skins you choose.

**A new 30-second trailer in full 1080p**, for phones (`trailer_phone.mp4`) and widescreen (`trailer_1080p.mp4`): 30-critter brawls on all three maps, the toy train ploughing through a battle, a rocket into a huddle in slow motion, mutated carrots, a nemesis and its gang, and the rewind.

Also in this release: paws that close round the guns' handles for every critter, idle animations, and automatic updates (the main menu tells you when a newer version is out).
