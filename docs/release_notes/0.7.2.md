## Bunny Beat Down v0.7.2: outfits that fit

**Every rogue's outfit has been refitted**, piece by piece, species by species. Hats are centred on the head and sized to the skull (a cap comes down to the brow, a top hat sits up top, a beanie pulls right down) instead of floating or swallowing faces; glasses sit over the eyes, even a squirrel's deep-set ones; bandanas cover the snout the right way up instead of hanging off like a bib; scarves go round the neck under the chin; backpacks face the right way and sit on the back at a size that suits the animal.

**Capes hang from the shoulders and move with the body**: a cape now bends with its wearer's back, from the shoulders down to the hips, instead of hovering behind the head like a board, and stops above a fox's or a squirrel's tail.

**Squirrels, raccoons and hamsters in particular** look much tidier: Nutsy swaps his old headphones for a sweatband and scarf, a hamster's ears no longer make its hat too big, and a raccoon's huge head no longer makes its bandana enormous. Beavers' tall heads now get properly covered by beanies and helmets.
