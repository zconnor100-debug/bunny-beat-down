## Bunny Beat Down v0.9.1: the costume pass, and a graphics check

- **Glasses sit on the eyes**, not the nose: fitted to each species' real eyes (foxes and beavers get bigger lenses to cover their big cartoon eyes); goggles are sized by their lenses; Twitch's, Ember's, Foxy Roxy's, Vixen Vera's, Gnawman's and Dr. Splash's all fixed.
- **Squirrels have proper eyes**: white eyeballs with dark pupils, sitting in their sockets instead of dark holes.
- **Hats** moved, tilted, turned and resized where they needed it: Sly, Fennick, Timber, Trash Panda, Grimsby, Dumpster Dot, Bandit Bill, Lady Garbage, Hops McGee, Hopscotch, Lily Pad Lou, Puddles, Croaker, Whisker Biscuit, Buck Toothington, Captain Carrot, Kernel, Hazel, Chompers' helmet (now covers his face), Bog Boss's shako (chin strap on the chin), Duchess's bearskin (strap under her chin, eyes showing), Sir Scamper's cavalier hat (centred, brim lined underneath).
- **New pieces**: a proper cloth headband (Rocco's is blue now), a ninja headband, a curly moustache, a monocle that sits on the eye, gold hoop earrings through the ear, shoulder pads, a big ruby necklace, neckerchiefs and cowboy-style bandanas, a new beret, a red bow tie for Pudge and a black one for Sir Reynard.
- **Bandit bunnies** are hooded, so their new looks are on the body: shoulder pads, earrings, bow ties; Vex gets a ninja headband and a bigger bandana over his hood.
- **Backpacks are worn**: straps over the shoulders and down the front.
- **Painted-on clothes**: Turbo's yellow T-shirt with blue lightning bolts, Croaker's leather chaps.
- **Capes** sit over a squirrel's tail instead of the tail poking through.
- No more bibs: Nutsy wears a cowboy-style bandana, Squeaks a bandana over his mouth.
- **Second round** (10 more notes): bigger shoulder pads on the bandit bunnies; Lop-Eared Larry's beanie covers his whole hood; Buck Toothington's monocle covers his eye; Gnawman's sunglasses and Hazel's hat are bigger (her eyes still show); Chompers' helmet sits further forward; Rocco's headband sits a little lower with the knot angled down at the back; Squeaks' backpack sits snug on his back; his and Nibbler's bandanas lie on their faces instead of jutting out like a beak; no more white seams on Nutsy's fur (or any squirrel's); and the eyelashes are gone.
- **Third round** (35 notes): necklaces worn like necklaces (the sapphire's pendant and Lady Garbage's hang on the chest facing forward, Vixen Vera's jewels lie on her chest down to just below her arms, the silver chain is thick enough to see); backpacks lean with the back and sit against it (Trash Panda, Acorn Al, Lodge Lady, Larry); Chompers' great helm turned straight so the cross is on his face; Bog Boss's shako sits clean between his eyes; Duchess's chin strap goes under her chin; Sir Toadsworth's monocle on his left eye, against it; Sir Scamper's cape drapes back over his tail; squirrels' eyeballs fill their sockets; Nutsy's bandana covers his nose and mouth; and nudges for Captain Carrot, Vex, Buck Toothington, Gulp, Turbo, Bushytail Betty, Sir Reynard, Larry, Nibbler, Squeaks and the bandit bunnies' shoulder pads.
- **The wardrobe on a beaver**: the ear-flap beanie, flower crown, Santa hat (its tip now flops back, on everyone), fedora, witch's and jester's hats, snorkel mask and the heart, 3D and pixel glasses all fitted to a beaver's big head; the laurel wreath turned so its leaves meet at the front; wings, the jetpack and the wind-up key are bigger.

**Rogue voices, second pass** (the owner's notes on all 49): 31 voices retuned: most slowed so every word is clear, the squeakiest (Nutsy, Turbo, Cheeks McGee) and the over-deep (Sir Toadsworth) brought back toward natural, robotic-sounding ones recast with Kokoro's best voice packs (Cottontail Kate, Foxy Roxy, Duchess now British, Kernel, Chompers, Dr. Splash, Larry), Gulp no longer Bog Boss's twin, Gnawman with a French speaker blended in, and misread lines fixed ("amateurs", Twitch's "WAH!", Puddles' "sorry").

**Name tags shrink with distance**: full size up close, smaller further off, and a second line (a gang's name) goes above the name, so a far-away critter is never hidden under its own tag.

**Graphics check** (Settings → Test graphics): a little live scene drawn exactly like a match, with the frame rate, the graphics API and card in use, quality and anti-aliasing to flip through, and one-click restarts into Direct3D 12 or the Compatibility renderer that come straight back to the check, to find what looks best on your machine.

**Your outfit never hides your crosshair**: anything you're wearing that's between the camera and the crosshair (a mortarboard on a raccoon's big head, say) fades see-through while it's in the way. And the sapphire and silver ring necklaces now sit around your neck instead of on your chest.

**A proper installer for Windows**: `BunnyBeatDown-Setup-windows-x86_64.exe` installs the game (no admin rights needed) and puts a Bunny Beat Down shortcut, with Bun as its icon, on your desktop and in the Start menu, with an uninstaller in Settings → Apps. The single-file zip is still there. On Linux, the download is a `BunnyBeatDown` folder whose `install.sh` adds a desktop launcher and a menu entry.

**Update from the desktop icon**: right-click the Bunny Beat Down icon → **Update Bunny Beat Down** (on Windows 11, choose "Show more options" first; it's also in the Start menu). It checks for a newer version, downloads it, installs it over the old one (your saves stay) and starts the game. On Linux it's in the launcher's right-click menu in the dock and applications menu, and under Scripts on the GNOME desktop.
