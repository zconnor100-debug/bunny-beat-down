## Bunny Beat Down v0.17.0: the Kernel Hunt's Finale, The Starfall

**The Finale of the Kernel Hunt is playable, and the story's complete.** The Briar took all three Kernels down through the bedding and home, under the lake, to where the Starfall Acorn fell. By nightfall Boulder Bluff is glowing gold-green over the whole island. It opens on the Kernel Hunt's page once Foam at the Falls is done.

**By night.** The first night in the game: a starry sky with the Milky Way and the moon, moonlight, crickets and an owl, and the Bluff's light going up into the dark where you can see it from anywhere on the island.

**Cut the roots.** Three of the Briar's taproots have broken up out of the island, the Kernels' light pulsing along them toward the Bluff, and the Touched grow up out of the ground round their knots while you're near. Carrots barely scratch the bark; a blast does it real harm (you start with grenades, and a crate brings more, and a rocket launcher). Cut, a root withers grey and sinks back into the ground.

**Get inside the Briar.** The wall that closes in on every match comes up round Boulder Bluff and closes on it, the Touched pour down at you, and the Warren answers: twelve rogues, the ones who came for Brutus and the ones you unwound in the toy room, gliding down to fight beside you.

**The Briar Heart.** The Bluff heaves, and a fourth boss comes up out of it: a beating heart of thorny vine as big as the Big Red Barn, in the split husk of the Acorn, built entirely in code. Its three Kernels are shut in pods that open one at a time to drink the light: that's when to shoot. It flings Thorn Volleys, rips Root Bursts up out of the ground toward you (they throw you off your feet), and lashes a vine right round its foot (jump it). Break its pods and the Kernels fly free; break the last and it splits open down the front, its heart's out, and the Briar closes in tighter.

**The ending.** The light goes out of the Touched, all at once, and they sit down where they stand. The three Kernels come together over the husk, the husk closes round them, and the Starfall Acorn, whole again, rises off the Bluff and up into the stars until it's a new one, with Little Bru, Model No. 7 and Nibbles watching it go. Fifteen new lines, in every rogue's own voice.

**Rewards:**
- **The Starfall** (beat the Briar Heart in the Finale): the **Shooting stars** beat-down burst.
- **Nobody Pricked** (finish the Finale with all seven rangers standing): 500 acorns.
- The Warren Gazette's special edition: THE ACORN GOES HOME.
- The Briar Heart's story, four chapters, on the Trophy Room's Bosses page once you've faced it.

**More toys that come apart.** Like Block Castle, Brick City's towers now come down when you put a rocket into them (knock the bottom out and the tower topples), the log cabins come apart log by log (the roof comes down once both its gable ends have gone), the block forts' platforms, pillars and gates can be knocked apart, and the loose letter blocks go flying. Whatever stood on a piece comes down after it, and a falling toy bowls over whoever's underneath.

**Also:**
- The squirrel's eyes sit in their sockets now instead of bulging out of them, and the eye under its higher left brow sits higher to match (the lopsided look stays).
- Eight new sounds, made in code: the Heart's heartbeat and groan, thorns flung, roots ripping up out of the ground, a knot snapping, a pod opening, the Acorn's chime going home, and the woods by night.
- A boss's arrival cinematic stands further back for a boss as big as the Heart.
