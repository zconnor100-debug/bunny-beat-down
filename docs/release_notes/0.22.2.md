## Bunny Beat Down v0.22.2: Crackjaw's arms swing free, big fights run faster, and a trailer with every boss

**Crackjaw's arms are his own.** His sleeves were modelled fused to his coat, so every swing of his sabre dragged a great sheet of coat along with it. His arms are pieces of their own now, like a real nutcracker's, turning on their pegs, and his coat stays where it is. His sabre arm also turns about its own middle now (it used to pivot from a point behind it), and shots at it hit where it is.

**Big fights run faster**, most of all on slower processors:

- Particle bursts nobody would see aren't made at all: a bot's muzzle flash 200 metres off, a hit behind you. Two thirds of a match's bursts were off screen and half of them a long way off.
- Critters out of view are posed less often.

Measured on Low with every bot in the match: about 15% faster as the whole lobby lands, about 10% in the fighting.

**A new Boss Battles trailer** with all six bosses: Brutus's ground pound, Old Hoot gliding in across the moon and carrying one of you off, the Tin Tyrant's eye lasers, Crackjaw's Quick March and his fall like a felled pine, Nibbles galloping into a wall and sitting there dazed, and Noodle popping up out of the bedding under a crowd.
