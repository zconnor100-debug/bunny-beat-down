## Bunny Beat Down v0.21.0: Noodle moves like a ferret, Tube Town rattles, and long matches stay fast

**Big fights run up to three times faster.** Every carrot fired left a few muzzle sparks behind that never went away: one leftover per shot, piling up into the thousands as a match went on, so long fights got slower and slower (worst of all on slower PCs). They're cleaned up now. A Hamster Cage Battle Royale with 49 bots, measured on the same machine, went from 41 frames a second in the thick of the fighting to 142, and the slowest frames from 49 ms to 10 ms (no more frames over 33 ms). Bots also do less work: they look for loot only in the squares round them, think a little less often and steer every other tick when they're far from the camera, and a critter standing still on solid ground skips its physics step. Loot only spins on screen and only checks for someone picking it up while someone's in reach, glowing loot puts its light out far away, and sounds out of earshot aren't played at all.

**Noodle moves like a ferret now** (worked out from a clip of a real ferret jumping and curling up).

- **Her War Dance hops are a ferret's bound:** her front end lifts first while her hind paws stay down, she stretches out long and flat as a tube in the air, and she comes down nose first, front paws landing before her back end. Her back only arches as she gathers for the next hop.
- **Lying down,** she sinks low and long like a snake, front end first, flat out on her belly, and then **coils up** nose to tail. Asleep she breathes slow and deep, and twitches as she dreams: a paw paddling, a hind leg kicking, an ear, the tip of her tail.
- **Waking** with a start she jerks her head up, gets up front end first, and shakes herself off from her nose to the tip of her tail.
- **Bopped silly,** she splats flat out of her hole like a dropped noodle, legs out, head lolling. **Beaten,** her front and back go down together and she ends long and limp, half over on her side.
- Her whole length bends into a turn, and every move flows from one pose into the next instead of stopping at each.

**Tube Town is part of her fight.** Going by under the bedding, Noodle sets Tube Town's Habitrail pods and tubes rattling and clattering on their connectors (and the hamster's cardboard burrow rolls), so you can hear where she's headed. Hide in a tube and she comes up under it: **POP!**, it's knocked clean off its connectors and goes tumbling, along with whoever was inside, and comes down hard on anyone under it. Pods go too. Ferrets love a tube: she dooks about it.
