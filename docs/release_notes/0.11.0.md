## Bunny Beat Down v0.11.0: the giants arrive, and they have a story

**Every boss arrives as a cinematic.** As it lands, the camera leaves you: looking up from the ground as Brutus or the Tin Tyrant drops out of the sky (or at the bedding heaving where Nibbles is about to burst out), a jolt and a beat of slow motion as it hits, then up close on its face as its name comes up with the line the Warren knows it by, and its roar. Then the camera's yours again, turned to face it. Any button skips it, and Settings → Game turns it off.

**The rogues on your side shout about it**, in their own voices: its name as it lands ("It's Brutus! Somebody reset the tally!", "The Tin Tyrant! Who opened the lid?!", "It's Nibbles! Don't touch the foam!"), RUN! when a blow's coming down on them or you, "It's stunned! Hit it NOW!", "It's winding down! Shoot the key!", "Aim for the head!", and a cheer when it goes down. Thirteen new lines for each of the 49 rogues.

**The bosses have a story.** One midsummer night the Starfall Acorn fell on Wildwood and split three ways, and whatever ate, wound or drank a piece grew, and went wrong. The Trophy Room has a new **Bosses** page: how the giants came, and each boss's story in four chapters you open by facing it and beating it (the legend, how it happened, how to beat it, and where it's going), with your record against it: faced, beaten, still standing, last blows, on Hard, fastest, your damage. The loading screen tells the Warren's rumours about the boss you're about to meet.

**Beat each boss for a piece of it:**
- **Giant Slayer** (beat Brutus): the **Brutus coat**, matted grey and torn by claw rakes that glow red and beat like a heart.
- **Unwound** (beat the Tin Tyrant): the **Tin Tyrant gun skin**, riveted tin painted like an old toy, with a red light sweeping its seams.
- **Pest Control** (beat Nibbles): the **Rabid froth** beat-down burst.
- **The Big Three** (beat all three), **Still Standing** (beat one without going down), **A Hard Nut** (beat one on Hard).
- Three ladders: **Giant Killer** (5/10/25 bosses beaten), **The Last Blow** (land it 2/5/10 times) and **Titan Tenderizer** (25,000/50,000/100,000 damage), topped by the Starfall Acorn's gold-green tracers, muzzle flash and reticle.

**A new trailer**, "Boss Battles": all three arriving, Brutus's ground pound, the Tin Tyrant's lasers and wind-down, Nibbles's charge into a wall.

The whole story, and the quest line it's building toward, is in docs/LORE.md.
