## Bunny Beat Down v0.18.1: faster loading, and no waiting to drop in

**Wildwood Island loads in a little over half the time** (9.7 seconds down to 5.6 at Epic, on the machine it's made on). The rocks, logs, stumps and cottages had their collision worked out from their models every time an island grew, two seconds of every load: it's worked out once now, ahead of time. The critters' paths are joined up while the grass is being scattered instead of after it, and the grass goes down faster. Same seed, same island.

**Ready up while it grows.** Press **Ready** (A, Space or Enter) while the island's still growing, and you drop in the moment it's grown, without another press. Press it again to keep looking.

**No more hitch on Nibbles's first spit.** The first time Nibbles spat foam in a session, the fight froze for nearly half a second while the puddle's texture was drawn. It's drawn twenty times faster now, and it and the bosses' warning rings are drawn while a Boss Battle or a Kernel Hunt mission loads, not mid-fight.

**Old Hoot is a real threat now.** She was the one boss that hardly hurt anyone: her warnings went up so early that everyone was long gone by the time she struck. Now she follows her prey as she lines up a Snatch and the red ring only shows as she dives, the Talon Rake's strip shows halfway into her swoop, her feathers come down in bigger rings (and more of them), and it takes more shots to make her drop whoever she's carrying. Her blows hit as hard as the other bosses' do.

**Also:** the swamp's lily pads come out the same every time for the same seed (they were picked at random each time). And a bug-hunting pass played nine whole matches out back to back, every map and every mode, sped up, and found nothing else stuck or broken; a Battle Royale against 49 bots at Epic runs at 70 to 110 frames a second on an RTX 5080, with no frame over 50 ms after the first.
