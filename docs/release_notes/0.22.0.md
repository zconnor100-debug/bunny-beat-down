## Bunny Beat Down v0.22.0: every boss moves like a living thing

Noodle got her ferret moves last time. Now every other boss gets the same treatment: each move flows into the next instead of stopping at every pose, a move cut short eases on from wherever the boss was instead of snapping back, and each boss moves like what it is.

**Nibbles moves like a hamster now.**

- **On all fours.** It used to walk sat up on its haunches. Now it scurries on all fours, its little legs going in quick diagonal pairs, its round body bobbing and rolling and its rump wagging, its head held steady.
- **Sits up to sniff.** Stood still a moment, it sits up on its haunches and looks round, nose in the air. Every few seconds of scurrying it stops to sit up and sniff for you (not once it's frothing mad).
- **The Frenzied Charge is a gallop.** Head down, rump up and wiggling, a forepaw raking the bedding; then off flat out, bounding and rocking front to back, and pulling up short at the end with its forepaws braced. (It used to slide there without moving its legs.)
- **Dazed for real.** Run into a wall, it bounces off and sits down hard, head going round and round, stars and all; then it shakes it off and washes its face with its forepaws before it drops back down.
- **Rabid Bite:** it rears up on its hind legs, forepaws up and jaws wide, throws itself forward jaws first and shakes whatever it got. **Foam Spit:** it sits up, heaves the foam up, and spits it with a toss of its head. **Burrow:** it digs like mad, forepaws by turns, flinging bedding out behind it, dives in nose first, and bursts up out of the bedding standing tall. **Screech:** stood right up, claws out, shaking with rage.
- **Its death:** a last screech, a stagger, its front end goes out from under it and it rolls over onto its side, legs kicking weaker and weaker until it's still. No more tipping over stiff like a toy.
- Rabid all the time: teeth chattering, its nose going, its head jerking now and then.

**Brutus** flows from move to move, and his great ears swing on after him: whipping forward when he pounds the ground, flopping as he lands a leap. After a pound, a leap or a roar he's out of breath, chest and shoulders heaving, mouth open. He shakes his head as he roars, and he goes down like a felled oak: reeling from the last hit, staggering, dropping to his knees and over onto his face, his ears flopping down after him.

**Old Hoot** moves like an owl: her head snaps round to you and holds there. Perched, she bobs and sways it to size you up, tilts it right over now and then, and shakes out all her feathers after she lands. She bobs her head as she hoots. Shot out of the sky, she lies dazed with her head rolling and her wings twitching. Going down for good, her wings beat feebly until they stop.

**The toys** stay stiff, like toys. Crackjaw and the Tin Tyrant now ease from one move into the next instead of snapping, Crackjaw's tall hat wobbles on its peg as he stamps, and the Tin Tyrant's tin claws rattle.
