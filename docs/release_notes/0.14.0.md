## Bunny Beat Down v0.14.0: the Kernel Hunt's Act II, The Clockwork Decree

**Act II of the Kernel Hunt is playable, and it's in the toy room.** The first Kernel won't stop humming, and it hums at the House on the shore, so the Barn Rangers take it across the lake, in at the window and down onto the children's bedroom floor (Toy Box Bash). The toy box stands open: they didn't lock it. Critters are marching stiffly round the room with keys turning in their backs, and high on the bookshelf two red eyes are watching, their light sweeping the floor. It opens on the Kernel Hunt's page once you've finished Act I.

**Free the Wound.** Four rogues of the cast went into the House and never came back: Turbo, Trash Panda, Hopscotch and Twitch, Wound now, each holding a landmark with a few more round them. Beat one down and it doesn't go down: it winds down, kneeling, its key slowing. Hold interact by it to pull the key out of its back before it winds itself back up, and it's free, and fights with you from then on.

**Break his winding stations.** Three tin wind-up machines as big as a shed, painted like old toys: a riveted base, a drum turning in brass bands, gears grinding, a big key turning on the end and a red bulb blinking. While you're near, each winds another of the Wound out of its chute every so often. Tin takes the sting out of carrots, except on the drum, where the spring is, and a rocket does them in (a crate comes down with a launcher in it). Broken, they come apart: the drum rolls away, the gears fly, the key goes up and comes down somewhere, all of it physics.

**Storm Block Castle.** His. The Wound hold the courtyard; take it with none of them in the ring and the Warren's flag runs up over his castle. Then the eyes on the bookshelf find you, and the Tin Tyrant jumps, across the whole room, down in front of his castle, with his arrival cinematic.

**The ending.** When he falls, the key in his back slows and stops, a gold-green light comes out of the hole it turns in, and he shrinks in his own steam to a tin toy robot no taller than a bunny: Model No. 7, from the windowsill. The second Starfall Kernel comes out of his back. "He's... a toy. He's just a toy." "Model Number Seven. It says so on his foot." He gets up, turns to them, and does what he did for thirty years: three steps, a beep and sparks. Buck Toothington brings out the first Kernel, and the two pull at each other, then both the same way: "Toward the hamster cage." Fourteen new lines, in every rogue's own voice.

**Rewards:**
- **The Second Kernel** (beat the Tin Tyrant in Act II): the **Clockwork** beat-down burst, cogs and sparks.
- **Nobody Left Wound** (finish Act II with all seven rangers standing): 300 acorns.
- The Warren Gazette's special edition: THE TIN TYRANT IS A TOY AGAIN, with the photo.

**Also:**
- Five new sounds, made in code like the rest: a wind-up key's ratchet, a key pulled out with a boing, a winding station's ticking machinery, one coming apart, and Model No. 7's beep.
- The Tin Tyrant beaten in Act II counts toward his records and opens his story's chapters, as in a Boss Battle.
- Act I's burning rings and Act II's castle ring share the same code now.
- The test runner can run single tests (`-- kernel_hunt::act_two`).
