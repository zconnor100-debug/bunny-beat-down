## Bunny Beat Down v0.7.1: your wardrobe, and the rogues in person

**A wardrobe of your own** in the Workshop (in the Burrow): hats, glasses, necklaces and wings that no rogue wears. Pick a hat (an ear-flap beanie, a flower crown, a Santa hat, a fedora, a witch's or wizard's hat), something for your face (heart, 3D or pixel glasses), a necklace, and something on your back (butterfly wings or a jetpack). They fit whichever critter you play, and you can see yourself wearing them beside the list as you browse, before you spend an acorn. A hat takes the place of the acorn helmet.

**Some can only be earned**, as Milestone rewards: the mortarboard (win 3 matches), the jester's cap (beat down 30 different rogues), star glasses (25 sniper headshots), fairy wings (finish 3 runs) and dark wings (win 2 nemesis hunts).

**The Rogues' Gallery shows every rogue in person**: each stands on a little stand in its outfit, holding its favourite gun. Drag to spin them round.
