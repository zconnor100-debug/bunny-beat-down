## Bunny Beat Down v0.12.0: the Kernel Hunt begins, and the Gazette stops the presses

**The Kernel Hunt** is the story mode: player against environment, on its own page in the main menu. The Starfall Acorn's light is getting into the Warren's critters. The Warren calls them the **Touched**: any species, gold-green veins glowing through their fur, silent, coming in waves. Its acts lead to the boss fights; the first is playable now, and the rest are on its page, sealed.

**Prologue: Days Since Brutus.** The tally on the Big Red Barn's door says nine days since anyone saw Brutus. It has never got past nine. You start on the ground at the Barn with the **Barn Rangers**, seven rogues of the cast (Buck Toothington, Dam Dan, Hazel, Kernel, Duchess, Captain Carrot and Lily Pad Lou), each at a post round it. The Touched come out of the Carrot Patch in three waves, then round the sides too, and the last brings a Touched giant, a beaver the size of a shed. Every Touched standing in the Barn's yard wears the Barn down (its bar is along the top of the screen); lose it and they have it. Between waves the rangers patch it up and a crate comes down behind it. The rangers call it all in their own voices: "Look at their eyes... they're GLOWING.", "They're in the yard! Push them out!", "What did they FEED that one?!" (sixteen new lines for each of the 49 rogues).

**Hold the last wave and the ground shakes.** Dust goes up off Boulder Bluff, something roars, and the rangers turn to look. "What... was THAT?" "That came from Boulder Bluff." Then the tally on the door is rubbed out and chalked back to zero.

**Rewards:**
- **Days Since Brutus: 0** (hold the Barn): the **Touched coat**, the Touched's own glowing veins, to wear.
- **Every Ranger Home** (hold it with all seven rangers standing): 250 acorns.

**The Warren Gazette stops the presses.** Big news doesn't wait for the rogues' next match any more: beat a boss and a **special edition** is waiting back at the menu ("BRUTUS FALLS AT THE CARROT PATCH", "TIN TYRANT UNWOUND AT BLOCK CASTLE", "NIBBLES STOPPED AT LAST"). The front page has a **photo the game took as it fell**, printed in halftone like newsprint, and the story says who landed the last blow and who was still standing. One of them is quoted, and the paper points you to the chapter of the boss's story the win opened. Beat one again and it's "BRUTUS DOWN AGAIN", with how many times now and the fastest. Hold the Barn and it's "THE BARN HOLDS", with the tally back at zero in the photo.

The story so far, and where it goes, is in docs/LORE.md.
