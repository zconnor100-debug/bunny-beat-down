## Bunny Beat Down v0.10.0: Team Battles and Boss Battles

**Two new ways to play** (New match → Mode):

**Team Battle (25 v 25).** You and 24 bots (the Carrot Crew) against 25 (the Bramble Mob). Each team rides its own balloon (or toy plane) down its own side of the map and lands there. Your team wears blue name tags you can see through walls, a blue rim and blue dots on the map, and nobody can hurt their own side: carrots, blasts and clubs alike. The bots you've made radical (and your rivals) end up on the other team; the bots that jump with you are your squad and stick by you. The Briar still closes in, and the last team standing wins, whether you're still standing or not.

**Boss Battle (25 v 1).** You and 24 bots against the map's boss, ten critters tall. No Briar: it turns up 35 seconds after everyone lands (its bar counts it down), and every blow it throws is warned by a red ring filling up on the ground where it's about to land (or a red strip where it's about to charge). Get out, or jump as a shockwave lands and it rolls right under you. The bots fight it from range, circle it, aim for its head and get out from under its blows (the slow ones get caught). Below half health it's enraged. Your arsenal can be called in as soon as it turns up.
- **Brutus, the Meadow's Nightmare** (Wildwood Island): a hulking monster bunny with a deranged face. Ground Pound, Boulder Toss (a volley of rubble when he's enraged), a Leap that lands on you from across the map, a Grab (he bites bots' heads off), and a Roar that blows everyone back.
- **The Tin Tyrant, the Wind-Up Warlord** (Toy Box Bash): a wind-up tin robot. Eye lasers swept across the floor (cover blocks them), volleys of toy missiles, a Clamp Smash, and every so often a Wind-Down: its key runs out and it stands there taking double (its key nearly triple) until it winds itself back up.
- **Nibbles the Rabid** (Hamster Cage): the cage's own hamster, grown huge and rabid, with matted fur, blood round its mouth and paws, foam at the jaw and red eyes. Frenzied Charges (run it into a wall and it's dazed), a Rabid Bite, Foam Spit that leaves burning, slowing puddles, and once enraged it burrows into the bedding and comes up under you.

**New sounds**: Brutus's roar, Nibbles's screech, the Tin Tyrant's tin clank and eye lasers, and the thud of something ten critters tall landing.

The bosses are CC-BY models from Sketchfab (see Credits), given skeletons (or, for the robot, its own rig re-rested) and animated by code.
